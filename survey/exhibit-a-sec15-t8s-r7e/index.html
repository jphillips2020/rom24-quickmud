The description

Land situated in the Township of Bedford, County of Monroe, State of Michigan, described as: That part of the Northwest quarter of Section 15, Town 8 South, Range 7 East, Bedford Township, Monroe County, Michigan, described as follows: Commencing at a point 7.7 feet South 89 degrees 58 minutes 30 seconds West from the North one quarter corner of said Section 15; thence South 89 degrees 58 minutes 30 seconds West 157.98 feet; thence South 0 degrees 40 minutes West 249.95 feet; thence due East 160.88 feet; thence due North 250.00 feet to the Point of Beginning.

The point the description commences at is the Point of Beginning: running all four calls from it returns to within 0.0085 ft, so the deed closes essentially perfectly. It is a four-sided parcel, very slightly out of square — the west line leans 0°40′ west of south, which makes the south line 2.90 ft longer than the north line.

The built-in anchor is known to be wrong

The shape and dimensions are exact — they come straight out of the deed. The absolute position is not: it hangs entirely off the coordinate of the North 1/4 corner of Section 15, which had to be reconstructed from published PLSS township geometry because no survey or parcel service was reachable.

Checked against aerial imagery, that reconstruction lands the parcel roughly three lots away, on golf course ground — an error on the order of a few hundred feet. Close enough to confirm the right stretch of Erie Road, not close enough to be the parcel. Correct it below before using any coordinate here for anything.

Re-anchor and export

Everything is parameterised on one coordinate, so one good point fixes the whole plot. Easiest is to find the parcel on imagery and read off its road-side east corner — the parcel's north line runs along Erie Road and its east line sits on the section's quarter line, 7.7 ft west of the section corner. If you have the surveyed section corner itself, switch the mode and enter that instead.

How the corner was located

Longitude

Ida Township (T7S R7E) and Bedford Township (T8S R7E) both publish a centroid longitude of 83°35′19″W. Same range, so that is the range's centre line — the section line 3 miles east of its west boundary, which is the Section 15/16 line, Jackman Road. The geocode of the Bedford Township Government Center at 8100 Jackman Rd (−83.5861) agrees, and Temperance's CDP centroid falls one mile further east on Lewis Ave. Section 15 therefore runs Jackman to Lewis, and its North 1/4 corner is the midpoint of that mile.

Latitude

Ida Township is a regular 36 sq mi township, so its published centroid fixes the T7S/T8S line at 41.82208. Section 15 sits in the third tier of sections from the north, putting its north line 2 miles south of that.

Corroboration

A listing at 1573 W Erie Rd carries the legal description “SEC 15, T8S R7E, COM AT THE NW COR OF THE NW 1/4 SEC 15”, confirming Erie Road runs along the north line of Section 15.
